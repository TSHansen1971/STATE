The Logical layer defines how STATE Engineering operates independently of its concrete technical realization.

Its governing question is:

> **How is an authorized system-state transition specified, bounded, executed, verified, evidenced and accepted?**

Logical roles and operations remain stable even when the physical actor or tool used to realize them changes.
