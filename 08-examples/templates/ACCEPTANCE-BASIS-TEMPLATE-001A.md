## Nature

This is a **non-normative operational template**.

It is a reusable physical representation of existing STATE semantics.

**Canonical mapping:** information carried by WP-01 Transition Intent and Specification / Transition Contract and later referenced by WP-08 Acceptance Record

The template name does not create a new Work Product class.

## Template

```text
acceptance_basis_identity:
transition_identity:
required_claims:
  - claim:
    target:
    required_condition:
    verification_expectation:
    evidence_expectation:
decision_rule:
treatment_of_fail:
treatment_of_inconclusive:
authorized_threshold_owner:
amendment_authority:
effective_from:
```

The Acceptance Basis shall be established prospectively to the degree required before it is relied upon for PASS or ACCEPT.
