## Nature

This is a **non-normative operational template**.

It is a reusable physical representation of existing STATE semantics.

**Canonical mapping:** physical representation of the existing Tailoring Decision fields; not a canonical Work Product class

The template name does not create a new Work Product class.

## Template

```text
tailoring_decision_identity:
transition_scope:
governing_profile:
context_factors:
role_arrangement:
gate_realization:
work_product_representation:
verification_depth:
independence_depth:
evidence_depth:
environment_control:
work_package_or_coordination_model:
release_control:
escalation_and_retailoring_triggers:
tailoring_authority:
decision_rationale:
tailoring_invariants_preserved: TI-01 through TI-12
```

Tailoring may compress representation.

Tailoring may not delete required control semantics.
