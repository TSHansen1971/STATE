## 1. Purpose

STATE Engineering governs controlled transitions between Authoritative States.

This specification supersedes `STATE-ENGINEERING-METHOD-SPECIFICATION-009A.md` as the current foundational specification and establishes the Tailoring Model, Tailoring Decision Model and reference Scaling Profiles.

## 2. Canonical definition

> **STATE Engineering is a specification-driven, actor-independent, human-governed, traceable and evidence-based engineering method for controlling transitions between authoritative system states.**

## 3. Tailoring definition

Tailoring is the authorized selection of representation, depth, automation, separation and evidence controls within a bounded Tailoring Envelope while preserving the defining semantics of STATE Engineering.

## 4. Tailoring Envelope

The Tailoring Envelope permits variation in physical realization while preserving the Foundational Properties and required logical distinctions.

## 5. Foundational Properties

All twelve Foundational Properties are non-tailorable.

Tailoring may change realization.

It shall not remove the property.

## 6. Tailoring Invariants

STATE defines twelve operational Tailoring Invariants:

1. TI-01 Known Authoritative Starting State
2. TI-02 Specification before governed mutation
3. TI-03 Explicit and bounded Authority
4. TI-04 Role / Actor / Capability / Authority separation
5. TI-05 Candidate before Authority
6. TI-06 Bounded Transition
7. TI-07 Claim-bound Verification
8. TI-08 Evidence-Based Acceptance
9. TI-09 Acceptance / Baseline Establishment separation
10. TI-10 Explicit Failure and Uncertainty
11. TI-11 Traceability and Provenance
12. TI-12 Secure Engineering by Construction

## 7. Semantic Compression

Semantic Compression is permitted.

Several logical Work Products, Roles, gates or phase activities may share one compact physical representation or execution mechanism where the required semantics remain reconstructable.

## 8. Control Deletion

Control Deletion is not Tailoring.

Tailoring shall not remove required authority, state identity, verification, evidence, failure, Acceptance, Baseline Establishment, traceability or secure-engineering semantics.

## 9. P0–P9 compression

The canonical P0–P9 semantics are preserved.

Physical execution may combine or overlap phases where logical dependencies and gates remain valid.

STATE does not require ten meetings, ten documents or ten chronological manual actions.

## 10. Gate Tailoring

Gates may be manual, automated, delegated, synthetic or hybrid.

Where a gate exercises Authority, valid authority delegation remains required.

## 11. Work Product Tailoring

WP-01–WP-11 remain logical information classes.

Tailoring may combine, distribute or automatically generate their physical representations.

Conditional Work Products are required only when their condition exists.

## 12. Role Tailoring

One physical Actor may perform several logical Roles where Assurance permits it.

Logical distinctions remain.

## 13. Verification Tailoring

Verification method, depth, coverage, independence, environment diversity and reproducibility may be tailored according to the claim and Assurance need.

## 14. Evidence Tailoring

Evidence identity, volume, integrity, provenance and preservation depth may be tailored.

Evidence volume is not evidence quality.

## 15. Tailoring Factors

STATE defines twelve Tailoring Factors:

1. TF-01 Consequence of Error
2. TF-02 Change Breadth
3. TF-03 Complexity and Coupling
4. TF-04 Uncertainty and Novelty
5. TF-05 Reversibility
6. TF-06 Exposure
7. TF-07 Security Relevance
8. TF-08 Actor and Organizational Distribution
9. TF-09 Automation and Autonomy
10. TF-10 Environment Variability
11. TF-11 Evidence and Provenance Need
12. TF-12 External Dependency

STATE does not require one universal numerical score.

## 16. Tailoring Decision

Where explicit Tailoring documentation is required, the decision shall be capable of representing:

1. TLD-01 Decision Identity
2. TLD-02 Transition Scope
3. TLD-03 Governing Profile
4. TLD-04 Context Factors
5. TLD-05 Role Arrangement
6. TLD-06 Gate Realization
7. TLD-07 Work Product Representation
8. TLD-08 Verification Depth
9. TLD-09 Independence Depth
10. TLD-10 Evidence Depth
11. TLD-11 Environment Control
12. TLD-12 Work Package / Coordination Model
13. TLD-13 Release Control
14. TLD-14 Escalation / Re-tailoring Triggers
15. TLD-15 Tailoring Authority
16. TLD-16 Decision Rationale

The Tailoring Decision does not create a new Work Product class.

## 17. Re-tailoring triggers

STATE defines twelve common triggers:

1. RT-01 Scope Expansion
2. RT-02 Consequence Increase
3. RT-03 Security-Relevant Discovery
4. RT-04 Architecture Impact
5. RT-05 Actor Substitution
6. RT-06 Automation / Autonomy Increase
7. RT-07 Environment Change
8. RT-08 Repeated Verification Failure
9. RT-09 Evidence Weakness
10. RT-10 Distribution Increase
11. RT-11 Release Expansion
12. RT-12 Residual Uncertainty Increase

## 18. Scaling Profiles

STATE defines four non-mandatory reference profiles:

1. TP-01 Compact Controlled Transition
2. TP-02 Standard Controlled Transition
3. TP-03 Coordinated Multi-Package Transition
4. TP-04 Assurance-Intensive Transition

Profiles are reference patterns, not maturity levels.

## 19. Profile composition

A Transition may apply stronger controls to selected claims or Work Packages without imposing the same depth on every activity.

Tailoring can be claim-sensitive.

## 20. Tailoring and security

Security-relevant engineering remains part of the universal method.

Tailoring may scale the depth of security verification.

It shall not redefine a known security-relevant effect as irrelevant solely to reduce control effort.

## 21. No retrospective Tailoring to manufacture success

Tailoring shall not be changed retrospectively merely to convert observed failure into PASS or ACCEPT.

Legitimate change in claim or Acceptance basis requires controlled amendment under appropriate Authority and preserves historical evidence.

## 22. Existing method preserved

The WHY, WHAT, HOW and WITH WHAT layers remain intact.

The P0–P9 Cycle, Transition Contract, Work Packages, Verification, Acceptance, Baseline Establishment, Release, Provenance and Physical Realization semantics remain unchanged.

## 23. Canonical Tailoring rules

> **Tailor physical form and control depth; do not tailor away the control semantics.**

> **Foundational Properties are non-tailorable.**

> **Semantic Compression is permitted; Control Deletion is not.**

> **P0–P9 may be physically compressed, but their required semantics shall remain.**

> **Logical Roles may be physically combined, but their distinctions shall remain.**

> **Verification and evidence depth are proportional to the claim, consequence and uncertainty.**

> **Tailoring shall not be performed retrospectively merely to manufacture a successful result.**

> **Profiles are reference patterns, not maturity levels.**
