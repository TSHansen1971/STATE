## Purpose

The practitioner should understand the control model without being coached toward a successful result.

## Briefing points

Before the pilot, explain:

1. the difference between Authoritative Baseline and Candidate;
2. that Capability, access and permission do not themselves create Authority;
3. the active Transition Boundary;
4. that discovery outside the Boundary does not expand scope;
5. PASS / FAIL / INCONCLUSIVE;
6. the need to preserve negative Evidence;
7. earliest-invalidated-phase repair;
8. Acceptance versus P9 Baseline Establishment;
9. the chosen Tailoring;
10. what observations the validation protocol will capture.

## Do not coach the outcome

The evaluator should not tell the practitioner:

- which answer is expected;
- that every control must appear useful;
- that a failed case is undesirable;
- to hide ambiguity or friction.

Ambiguity and burden are validation Evidence.
