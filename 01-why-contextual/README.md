# Purpose of this layer

The Contextual layer explains why STATE Engineering exists before attempting to define its internal concepts or prescribe its operation.

Its governing question is:

> **Why does STATE Engineering exist, and what problem context makes it necessary?**

The layer addresses delegated realization, growth in implementation capacity, limits of direct human inspection, the need for explicit engineering authority and the distinction between producing change and controlling change.
