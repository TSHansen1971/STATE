## 1. Purpose

This document records licence provenance for authored STATE Engineering documentation.

It distinguishes the licence applied to the current corpus from licence grants attached to earlier revisions and historical release material.

## 2. Historical licence

The v1.0.0 release corpus and earlier published STATE documentation were distributed with the visible documentation licence:

`CC BY-NC-ND 4.0`

Historical release artifacts and Historical Superseded Specifications retain their original licence metadata as provenance.

## 3. Prospective v1.1 Candidate transition

Owner decision D3 selected:

`CC BY-SA 4.0`

WP19 implements that decision prospectively for current authored documentation beginning with the v1.1.0 Candidate corpus revision containing WP19.

The exact Git commit implementing this transition is recorded by the WP22 Release Candidate evidence after the Candidate commit is established.

The licence transition does not by itself make `v1.1.0` an accepted public release.

## 4. Earlier grants are not revoked

Earlier copies distributed under `CC BY-NC-ND 4.0` remain subject to the licence grant under which those copies were distributed.

The later `CC BY-SA 4.0` publication is not described as retroactively revoking earlier grants.

## 5. Forward licence rationale

The selected forward licence supports:

- broad legitimate redistribution;
- publication of adaptations and translations;
- commercial use;
- mandatory attribution;
- ShareAlike for distributed adaptations.

The canonical licence information is available from Creative Commons at:

`https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/`

## 6. Copyright precision

The documentation licence applies to copyrightable expression to the extent protected by applicable copyright law.

It does not purport to create copyright protection for ideas, facts, methods, systems, procedures or engineering concepts that are not protected by copyright.

Other rights may require separate consideration where applicable.

## 7. Release-history rule

A release record shall identify the licence of the corpus revision it binds.

A later licence choice shall not be used to rewrite the historical licence metadata of an immutable earlier release artifact.
