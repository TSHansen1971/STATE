## Purpose

Capture enough Evidence to evaluate both the engineering Transition and STATE's control behavior.

## Minimum capture

Preserve where applicable:

- Baseline identity;
- Specification / Transition Intent;
- Acceptance basis;
- Authority Grant;
- Actor Assignment;
- Transition Boundary;
- Tailoring decision;
- Candidate identity;
- material transformation record;
- execution observations;
- Verification Records;
- negative Evidence;
- Evidence Set / manifest;
- Acceptance decision;
- P9 Baseline Establishment;
- repair / resumption records;
- deviation / escalation records;
- elapsed control effort;
- practitioner ambiguity notes.

## AI / automation additions where relevant

Capture to claim-relevant depth:

- instruction or prompt reference;
- model/runtime identity;
- material generation parameters;
- tool actions;
- environment identity;
- generated/transformed artifact provenance.

Do not retain irrelevant private conversation merely because AI was present.

## Integrity

Each Evidence item should have a stable identity or preserved location.

Use digests where they materially strengthen artifact identity.

A digest does not replace provenance.

## Negative Evidence

Do not delete:

- failed tests;
- rejected Candidates;
- INCONCLUSIVE observations;
- boundary violations;
- failed repair attempts;
- practitioner confusion;
- evidence gaps.

These are part of the validation basis.
