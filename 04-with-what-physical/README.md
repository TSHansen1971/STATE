The Physical layer defines how the logical STATE method is realized in a concrete engineering environment.

Its governing question is:

> **With what people, teams, synthetic capacity, hardware, software and infrastructure is the logical method implemented?**

The Physical layer may include:

- individual engineers and engineering teams;
- local, distributed and externally sourced delivery capacity;
- specialist suppliers;
- AI models and autonomous engineering agents;
- hybrid human–synthetic teams;
- workstations, servers and computing infrastructure;
- operating systems and execution environments;
- source and version control;
- compilers, interpreters, SDKs and development toolchains;
- build and packaging systems;
- CI/CD and execution automation;
- verification and analysis tooling;
- evidence storage and logging;
- cryptographic identity and integrity mechanisms;
- release and distribution infrastructure.

Physical implementation choices shall not silently redefine logical authority.

A more capable realization actor may increase throughput without receiving additional authority unless that authority is explicitly delegated.
